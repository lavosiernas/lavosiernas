## Building the company. Engineering the details.

I'm Lavosier, a founder and full stack developer working at the intersection of **digital identity, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence**.

I lead the development of **Auth21** and its ecosystem, connecting product direction with the engineering behind it. My work spans authentication, agent runtimes, native applications, and cloud infrastructure.
